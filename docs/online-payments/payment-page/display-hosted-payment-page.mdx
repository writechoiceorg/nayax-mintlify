Once you have a signed URL, show it to your customer either in an iframe or as a full-page redirect.

## Show the payment page

You have two ways to show the signed URL to the shopper: embedded in an
iframe, or as a full-page redirect.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Iframe (recommended)">
    An iframe keeps the shopper on your page, avoids redirect blocking, and avoids losing the session to SameSite cookie restrictions. Embed it like this:

    ```html
    <iframe
      src="https://merchant-api.sb.nayaxpay.com/pp/{merchantId}?orderId=...&signature=..."
      allow="payment"
      width="100%"
      height="600"
      frameborder="0"
    ></iframe>
    ```

    `allow="payment"` is required. Without it, the browser blocks the Payment Request API, and the Apple Pay and Google Pay buttons don't appear.

    The Hosted Payment Page shows the order total, Apple Pay and Google Pay buttons, saved payment methods when you provide `userId`, and a card entry form for the [supported card networks](/docs/online-payments/supported-payment-methods). Wallet buttons appear automatically once Apple Pay or Google Pay is enabled for your account. Nayax also completes Apple Pay merchant validation server-side, so you don't need to run your own domain verification for this page.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Full-page redirect">
    A full-page redirect sends the shopper's browser directly to the signed URL. Use it where an iframe isn't practical, for example inside a webview. Navigate to it like this:

    ```js
    // Server-side: generate the signed URL, then navigate the browser
    window.location.href = "https://merchant-api.sb.nayaxpay.com/pp/{merchantId}?orderId=...&signature=...";
    ```

    On completion, Nayax redirects the shopper to your `returnURL`. The redirect doesn't include the result; you confirm the transaction's status server-side either way, covered next.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Language and locale

The Hosted Payment Page's language is determined by the `currency`
parameter; there's no separate language or locale parameter to set.

| `currency` | Page language |
| --- | --- |
| `ILS` | Hebrew (RTL layout) |
| All other currencies | English (default) |

## 3D Secure (3DS2)

When 3-D Secure 2 (3DS2) is enabled for your account, the Hosted Payment
Page authenticates the cardholder automatically. You don't need any
additional integration for this. 3DS2 runs one of two ways.

| Flow | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Frictionless | Authentication completes invisibly. The shopper sees no extra step. |
| Challenge | The issuer requires more proof. The page shows the challenge inline and completes the payment once the cardholder responds. The shopper never leaves your checkout. |

Either way, the outcome reaches you through the result object and webhooks
described in
[Receive the payment result](/docs/online-payments/payment-page/receive-payment-result).

A successful authentication satisfies PSD2 Strong Customer Authentication
(SCA), and shifts liability for fraud-related chargebacks to the issuer
where the card scheme allows it.

For eligible EEA and UK merchants, Nayax can apply an SCA exemption, for
example based on transaction-risk analysis or a low transaction value. This
lets you authorize a low-risk payment without a challenge.

Apple Pay and Google Pay use their own network authentication, so they
never trigger a 3DS challenge.

Once your customer sees the Hosted Payment Page and completes the payment, move on to [receive the payment result](/docs/online-payments/payment-page/receive-payment-result).
