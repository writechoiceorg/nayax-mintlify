The Hosted Payment Page integration has three steps: build a signed URL, show the page to your customer, and receive the result. Your first milestone is getting a valid `postMessage` response back after a test payment.

## How the flow works

A Hosted Payment Page transaction moves through four actors: your server, the shopper's browser, the Nayax gateway, and the acquirer or card issuer. From your side, it's a three-step flow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build a signed URL">
    Your server creates a URL that encodes the order details (amount, currency, order ID) and signs it with your `merchantSigningSecret`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Display the Hosted Payment Page">
    Your site shows that URL to the shopper, either in an iframe or as a full-page redirect. The shopper enters their card or picks a digital wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive the result">
    Nayax authorizes the transaction with the acquirer and sends the result back through `postMessage` (iframe mode), a redirect to your `returnURL`, or both.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Behind that, the gateway verifies your signature, replaces the card or wallet details with a secure token, and authorizes the transaction with the acquirer before returning a result to the shopper's browser.

## Confirm the result server-side

The `postMessage` response and the `returnURL` redirect tell your front end how to update the shopper's screen. Neither one is proof of payment.

<Warning>
  Treat `postMessage` and `returnURL` as UX signals only. Before you fulfill an order, confirm the transaction's final status server-side by calling [Get transaction](/docs/online-payments/rest-api/transactions#get-transaction). Don't rely on the browser result alone.
</Warning>

## What you need

Building your first signed request needs two credentials from your sandbox onboarding.

- `merchantId`: a UUID Nayax provisions per environment.
- `merchantSigningSecret`: provisioned per environment, server-side only. Never expose it in client-side code.

If you haven't completed onboarding yet, see [Prerequisites and onboarding](/docs/online-payments/prerequisites-and-onboarding). Complete production onboarding before you switch to production credentials.
