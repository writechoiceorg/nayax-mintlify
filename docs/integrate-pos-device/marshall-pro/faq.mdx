# Connecting the device to your POS/ simulator

To establish a link with the device, TCP protocol should be used through port 5050 (no physical connection to your computer is required).

Regarding the setup of your device's Wifi/LAN connection to your local network, please refer to the following resources:

Wifi connection: [https://devzone.nayax.com/docs/integrate-pos-device/marshall/marshall-faqs/faq#configuring-wifi-and-ip](https://devzone.nayax.com/docs/integrate-pos-device/marshall/marshall-faqs/faq#configuring-wifi-and-ip)<br />LAN connection: [https://devzone.nayax.com/docs/integrate-pos-device/marshall/marshall-faqs/faq#configuring-eth-and-ip](https://devzone.nayax.com/docs/integrate-pos-device/marshall/marshall-faqs/faq#configuring-eth-and-ip)

# Triggering apps update process (accessing CasHub's app)

Once Nayax Fuel's Support team has submitted the relevant app update request to the device, please turn on the device and proceed with one of the following two options:

1. Leave the device powered on overnight: it will reset automatically at approximately 3-4 AM, at which point the updates will be collected and initiated.
2. Manually access the CASHUB app to trigger the download and installation of the updates.

If you select the second option, please proceed as follows:

First, set the device to "Out of Service" status by disconnecting the link to the device and waiting 30 seconds:

Please note that certain apps can only be downloaded, not installed, at this stage, as the device cannot update applications that are currently running. This applies to apps such as

- Nayax Launcher
- Update Service
- VPOS Service

For these, the device must be rebooted afterward, and the apps will be updated during the device's power-up sequence. <br />During this process, a "Castles: Updating (application)" notification will appear for approximately one minute.
